## Before you start

- **Best tools:** ChatGPT (image generation) or Gemini — both handle tall images, keep a consistent style, and accept a
  reference image. Midjourney is great for the background scenes but weaker at transparent PNGs.
- **Keep the set consistent.** Upload your finished #1 alongside Part A prompts, and your finished #7 alongside the
  rest of Part B, and add: *"Match the painting style, colours and level of detail of the attached image."*
- **No text in images.** AI garbles lettering. All names, dates and headings are added by the website on top.
- **Transparent = transparent.** Where a prompt says *transparent background*, ask for a PNG with transparency
  (ChatGPT supports this). If your tool can't, ask for a **flat pure white background** and tell me — I'll cut it out.
- **Size:** tall phone format unless noted. Aim for at least 1080 px wide. Bigger is fine; I'll compress.

The set has two moods:
**Part A (top of site)** — bright sunlit sky and colourful temple. ✅ Done (except #6).
